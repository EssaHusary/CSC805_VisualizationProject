Regional Shifts of Baby Names (1910–2025)

Each of the 51 Census areas (50 states + DC) belongs to exactly one official Census region. Four regions pull on one rope; a region's pull is the rate at which babies there received the name, normalised by how many babies the region had at all. The knot settles at the balance point.
